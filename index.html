02 / The object

Small object.
Big energy.

Born from a simple idea: the things around your coffee deserve as much attention as the coffee itself. ORYZO treats the coaster as a piece of product design, not an afterthought.

WHY ORYZO

Quietly over-engineered.

Natural cork gives the surface a warm tactility while creating a soft buffer between a hot cup and the table beneath it. The result is simple, tactile and intentionally a little ridiculous.

No app. No account. No firmware update.
